# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses, and where staff look after the money that flows back to learners.

## Language

### Money back

**Purchase**:
A learner buying a course once, for the amount they paid. Every purchase stands on its own, even when the same learner buys the same course again.

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get the full amount of one Purchase back, decided by an admin. The canonical term for everything in this area.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Cancel (as a domain term), Money back, Chargeback

**Pending refund**:
A Refund that has been requested and not yet decided.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, open request

**Approved refund**:
A Refund an admin has accepted. The learner gets back the amount they paid and loses access to the course. The decision is final for that Purchase.

**Declined refund**:
A Refund an admin has turned down. The decision is final for that Purchase.

**Refund eligibility**:
The single policy deciding whether a Purchase may be refunded: within 14 calendar days of purchase, less than 2 hours watched, and less than 50% of lessons completed. It is also unavailable for a claimed Seat. A Team licence can be refunded only as a whole, and only while no Seat is claimed.

**Decline reason**:
The explanation an admin must give when declining a Refund, shown to the learner.

**Team licence**:
A single Purchase made by a team admin that provides several seats.

**Seat**:
One learner's place within a Team licence, claimed by redeeming a coupon. A claimed seat belongs to the team, so its learner cannot request a Refund.

**Access**:
A learner's ability to open a course's lessons. An approved Refund removes Access, but the learner's progress, quiz attempts, ratings and comments are kept and return if they buy the course again.

**Admin**:
The staff member who approves or declines a Refund. Instructors do not decide Refunds.

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a future case where a bank forces money back. It is not a Refund and does not exist in the product yet.
